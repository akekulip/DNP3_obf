Why a bridge, and the two ways to build it (to be confirmed with Dr. Lin — do not assume topology)

A 10/100 RJ45 relay cannot plug into a 25–40G Tofino port. Something has to move the relay's traffic onto the high-speed fabric so it traverses the switch:

  • A · Host bridge (recommended). Relay → normal switch → Hulk (spare RJ45 NIC) → Hulk L2-bridges it onto its 25G link into the Tofino (dp8/dp9, already proven). Reuses existing, working hardware.
  • B · Switch uplink + VLANs. Relay stays on the normal switch; a 10G uplink from that switch to the Tofino carries the relay VLAN, and VLANs force master↔relay to pass through the Tofino. Needs a 10G-capable uplink + VLAN config on the normal switch.

Either way, the only L2 path from Vision to the SEL-751 must run through the Tofino — otherwise the normal switch would short-circuit the obfuscation.
