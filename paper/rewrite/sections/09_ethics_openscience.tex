\section*{Ethics Considerations}

This work required no human subjects and no personal data. The experiments ran on a laboratory
testbed we own: one SEL-751A protective relay, one Intel Tofino-1 switch and one master host, on
an isolated network with no connection to any operational system. No production grid, utility
network or third-party device was contacted at any point.

The relay is physical equipment whose outputs can operate a breaker, so the control experiments
were constrained accordingly. Every select-before-operate exchange in the evaluation addresses
isolated control points through a guarded driver, and no control operation in this paper moved a
breaker or energized a circuit. Every OPERATE in the evaluation is a protocol exchange the relay accepted and acknowledged, not
a physical actuation.

The mechanism we describe is defensive. It removes a timing feature that an adversary would use
for reconnaissance, and it neither creates a new attack nor discloses a vulnerability in the
relay, the switch or the DNP3 protocol. We report one effect that works against the defender, and
we report it in full rather than omit it: holding the acknowledgment makes the
request-to-acknowledgment interval more informative than it was, from 0.379 to 0.662 balanced
accuracy for an adversary that retrains. An operator weighing deployment needs that number, so
withholding it would have been the greater harm.

\section*{Open Science}

The evidence and the analysis are reproducible from the raw captures. The packet captures, the extraction and statistics code, the figure generators, the pinned environment and the tests
are archived together, and a single script rebuilds every number and every figure in this paper
from the captures. A hash manifest covers each published artefact, and a publication gate
compares a fresh rebuild against what the paper contains, so a discrepancy between the manuscript
and the evidence fails rather than passes silently.

The switch program is the exact P4 source that ran, retained with its compilation record. We do
not release the relay's firmware or vendor documentation, which are not ours to distribute. On
acceptance we will publish the captures, the analysis pipeline and the switch source under an
open license; the artifact is prepared for evaluation in the form described here.
