\section*{Open Science}

The evidence and the analysis are reproducible from the raw captures, archived with the extraction
and statistics code, the figure generators, the pinned environment and the tests. One script
rebuilds every number and the campaign figures; the histogram and model figures have their own
generators and the manuscript its own build. A hash manifest covers each published artefact, and a
publication gate compares a fresh rebuild against what the paper contains, so a discrepancy fails
rather than passing silently. That gate compares rendered
PDFs byte for byte, which is stricter than numerical agreement: the numbers reproduce wherever the
pinned environment installs, while the last bits of a plotted coordinate need not agree across
interpreter builds. The figures the page budget keeps out of the manuscript are published too.

The switch program is the exact P4 source that ran, with its compilation record; the relay's
firmware and vendor documentation are not ours to distribute. On acceptance the captures, the
analysis pipeline and the switch source are published under an open license.

\section*{Ethics Considerations}

This work required no human subjects and no personal data. The experiments ran on a laboratory
testbed we own: one SEL-751A protective relay, one Intel Tofino-1 switch and one master host, on
an isolated network with no connection to any operational system. No production grid, utility
network or third-party device was contacted at any point.

The relay is physical equipment whose outputs can operate a breaker, so every
select-before-operate exchange in the evaluation addresses isolated control points through a
guarded driver. No control operation in this paper moved a breaker or energized a circuit: each
OPERATE is a protocol exchange the relay accepted and acknowledged, not a physical actuation.

The mechanism we describe is defensive. It removes a timing feature that an adversary would use
for reconnaissance, and it neither creates a new attack nor discloses a vulnerability in the
relay, the switch or the DNP3 protocol. We report one effect that works against the defender, and
we report it in full rather than omit it: holding the acknowledgment makes the
request-to-acknowledgment interval more informative than it was, from 0.380 to 0.662 balanced
accuracy for an adversary that retrains. An operator weighing deployment needs that number.
