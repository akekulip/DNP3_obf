Release timeline of NOMAD's Timing strategy at D = 10 ms, J = 10 ms. (a) READ: the switch forwards the request, holds the outstation's acknowledgment until da after the request (31 ms) and releases the response D after the acknowledgment's actual release, so the CLRT the master sees is D. (b) OPERATE: the switch first holds the command for J before delivering it; its acknowledgment and response are then held exactly as in (a), so the master-visible timing does not depend on J.
