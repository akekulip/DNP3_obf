\textbf{Measured interval per transaction class, over 22 grouped runs.} (a) READ and (b) the SELECT phase of SBO report the cross-layer response time; (c) OPERATE reports the master-visible response-to-ACK interval, which is a different anchor and not a complete SBO transaction.
