## Summary of the draft (the paragraph a reviewer writes first)

The draft asks whether a DNP3 control link can be reshaped so the observable wire transcript `O = [(direction, size, release_time, outer_header)_i]` is independent of the protected device and response (`P(O|X=x,C=c)=P(O|X=x',C=c)`), provably on Tofino-1 hardware, building on a frozen upstream timing result (Defense 4: CLRT normalization, 240/240 held, 0 bypass, one SEL-751, READ-only). It surveys seven candidate architectures, and its own specialist analyses converge on a single recommendation: the fixed-transcript property is **unreachable single-edge in plaintext**, so it requires **paired encrypting software gateways** (one at the outstation, one at the master) owning an encrypted fixed-cell envelope, with the Tofino demoted to a **metronome (grid release) + header-scrubber** kept outside the crypto trust boundary — i.e. Ditto's shape with a software peer, or IP-TFS (RFC 9347) realized at a substation crypto gateway. The proof machinery (functional correctness, transcript invariance-as-equivalence, privacy-failure accounting) and the honesty about residual channels are unusually strong. **The problem is that the draft's own logic dissolves the headline contribution:** the property it wants to claim as novel lives in a design cell (single-edge, plaintext, endpoint-preserving) that the same draft proves is infeasible, and the feasible cell it retreats to is off-the-shelf IP-TFS plus a Tofino whose sole unique value is unmeasured.

## Strengths (stated honestly)

1. **The evidence discipline is genuinely top-tier for a self-review.** The `EVIDENCE_LEDGER` re-derives the D4 240/240 result from raw `blocks.jsonl` rather than trusting prose, catches the stale-CHARTER "defect OPEN" contradiction, flags the two-different-datasets seam (A2), and the p=0.0769 permutation-null floor (A4). The `PROOF_OBLIGATIONS` insistence on equivalence testing (TOST, not non-significance), the analytic `1/C` + Bayes-optimal chance baseline (rejecting the classifier fold-majority number), and PATTERN_NORMAL coverage in the headline are exactly the corrections a hostile reviewer would demand — and they are already in.
2. **The unavoidability chain is well-constructed and, for the strong property, essentially airtight.** Protocol self-description + observation-point topology + no data-plane cipher is a real argument, not hand-waving, and it is cross-checked against measured facts (`ip.len` unmoved at 1.000 bits under trailer padding; payload never enters the PHV).
3. **The residual channels are named, not buried.** B5e (endpoint-stamped headers) and the request-direction leak are declared expected-FAILs up front (`PROOF_OBLIGATIONS §B.2`, `EXPERIMENT_PLAN §7`), which is the opposite of the median-over-a-mixture failure the project already suffered once.
4. **The Tofino feasibility analysis is concrete and falsifiable** (work-conserving skip proven on silicon; no-L1-layer proven three ways from SDE source; the cadence risk isolated as R13 rather than assumed away).

## Ranked objections (most-lethal first)

### O1 — MUST-FIX (lethal to the contribution). The recommended design collapses to "IP-TFS between two gateways + a Tofino of unproven value," and the draft proves this itself.

- **Claim attacked:** the project's headline novelty — a single-edge, plaintext-safe, endpoint-preserving DNP3 fixed transcript (`PRIOR_WORK_MATRIX.md` Novelty candidate Z.1/Z.2; `README`/`RESEARCH_CHARTER` framing).
- **Why it is wrong:** the transport analysis (`TRANSPORT_AND_ENCRYPTION_OPTIONS.md §0, §2, §5`) and R1/R14 establish that a fixed transcript **requires encryption and a decrypting peer** — Architecture 4, paired software gateways with split-TCP termination. Once you accept that (and the argument is sound), every "genuinely new" item in the novelty candidate evaporates:
  - Z.1 "plaintext-safe CRC-valid chaff" — killed by R14/§2.2 (plaintext chaff is detectable or deletes relay SOE). The design uses ciphertext chaff after all — that is Ditto/IP-TFS.
  - Z.2 "single-edge, endpoint-preserving" — killed by the unavoidability result. The design uses two endpoints after all — that is Ditto/IP-TFS.
  - Z.3 "separate-ACK CLRT normalization" — this is the **frozen upstream Defense 4**, not new here; and a continuous constant-rate grid makes release times content-independent by construction, subsuming CLRT.
  - Z.4 "live outer-header synthesis" — under the split-TCP termination the transport doc itself recommends (`§4.1`, `§7`), the gateway emits the canonical outer connection and the inner TSval/`data_offset` never reach the WAN. Header canonicalization is done by the tunnel endpoint — exactly what IP-TFS/ESP give "for free." The Tofino header-scrub (N1/N2/N3) is then **redundant with the gateway**, needed only for the in-band variant the doc de-prioritizes.
  - Z.5 "safety-bounded fixed schedule" — a rate/parameter choice already covered by IP-TFS's congestion-controlled mode; not a mechanism.
  What remains that off-the-shelf IP-TFS-between-two-gateways does not already give is only: (i) line-rate low-jitter release on the switch instead of a software pacer, and (ii) DNP3-aware eligibility. (i) is **R13, unmeasured**; (ii) largely dissolves once TCP is terminated at the gateway and cover is continuous. The literature confirms the deployment context makes this worse for novelty: substation crypto gateways encapsulating DNP3 in IPsec/TLS are **standard practice**, and constant-rate/fragmenting TFC over IPsec is a solved, standardized capability (RFC 9347; Király/Teofili TFC-in-IPsec, 2007).
- **What would answer it:** either (a) retire R13 with a positive silicon measurement showing the Tofino metronome delivers a release-jitter/latency profile a software IP-TFS pacer provably cannot, within the DNP3 timing budget — and reposition the entire contribution around that measured switch-vs-software delta; or (b) drop the "novel fixed transcript" framing and reposition around the impossibility result (O7). As written, the novel intersection is the design cell the draft simultaneously proves infeasible — you cannot claim novelty for occupying a cell you proved is empty because it is impossible.

### O2 — MUST-FIX (decision-critical, technical). The Tofino's only unique value is the one quantity that is unproven; the "proven timing primitive" is a different mechanism from the one the design needs.

- **Claim attacked:** that the Tofino can enforce the fixed release grid (R-time) on silicon (`ARCHITECTURE_CANDIDATES.md` Candidate A/E; `TOFINO_TM_FEASIBILITY.md §0, §9A`).
- **Why it might be wrong:** the fixed transcript is a **cadence** property. TF1's only rate primitive is a max-rate shaper that is silicon-measured to **clump below ~600 pps**; the pktgen periodic-timer floor/jitter is **undocumented** and only a steady ~100 pps is measured (R13; `TOFINO_TM_FEASIBILITY §11`). Crucially, the silicon-proven D4 primitive is **event-anchored deadline release** (`t_wire = min{τ_i ≥ t_eligible}`, p5–p95 spread 0.05–0.12 ms) — a *reactive, per-transaction* release, **not** a free-running metronome grid that emits cells whether or not real data exists. The design needs the metronome; the metronome is exactly what is unmeasured. The draft must not cite the D4 deadline result as evidence the grid works — they are different mechanisms.
- **Does R13 alone force NO_GO?** No — it forces GO_WITH_BOUNDED_CLAIM and makes a silicon cadence characterization the mandatory first hardware probe. But note the sting: if the Tofino cadence is poor, the **software gateway can pace the grid** (software constant-rate pacing is routine), which removes the Tofino's reason to exist and feeds O1. So R13 is lethal to the *Tofino-centered framing*, not to the defense.
- **What would answer it:** a pre-build silicon measurement of the pktgen periodic-timer grid (jitter, IPG floor, max clean rate) with a pass/fail bound tied to the DNP3 latency budget, and a head-to-head against a software pacer.

### O3 — MUST-FIX (internal consistency). Two documents assert opposite novelty premises; the decision memo would inherit a false claim.

- **Claim attacked:** the novelty framing in `PRIOR_WORK_MATRIX.md` ("Plaintext-safe chaff… Single-edge, endpoint-preserving") versus `TRANSPORT_AND_ENCRYPTION_OPTIONS.md §2` + R14.
- **Why it is wrong:** these are direct contradictions on the load-bearing claim. §2.2 shows plaintext CRC-valid chaff is either detectable (bad CRC/illegal frame) or dangerous — `ProcessIIN` runs even on rejected frames; a fabricated CONFIRM makes the relay permanently delete SOE records. That is a safety disqualification, not a privacy trade-off. **The transport analysis is right; the PRIOR_WORK novelty section is unsound.**
- **Salvage (attack point 3, answered):** essentially none as a *privacy* mechanism. The only survivors are narrow and mostly out of scope: request canonicalization / S6 (make the relay genuinely answer a fixed maximal point set — not strippable because the relay truly produces it, but it changes what the relay reports and may be operationally unacceptable), and decoy *READs* (valid, but they still elicit the real device's real headers/timing, so they do not hide identity and they load a protection relay). Plaintext chaff must be struck from the novelty claim.
- **What would answer it:** reconcile the two documents before the decision memo; delete Z.1/Z.2 from the novelty claim; state that the sound design is the paired-gateway encrypted one and the single-edge/no-crypto version is the impossibility floor (Architecture 5), not a defense.

### O4 — MUST-FIX (evaluation). On current data the evaluation supports **no affirmative invariance claim at all**, and the two decision-critical probes are unrun.

- **Claim attacked:** that the evaluation can support any bounded privacy claim now (`EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`; `PROOF_OBLIGATIONS §B.3`).
- **Why it is wrong:** (i) six flows, three labels → the flow-level permutation null **bottoms at p = 7/91 = 0.0769** and can never reach significance (A4; `EXPERIMENT_PLAN §2.4, §4`); (ii) **one physical unit per model**, so every 1.000 is "these three units, this config," not a device-family claim (`§4`); (iii) the size evidence (10.0.0.x corpus, CLRT median 12.21 ms) and the timing proof (physical .7, ~1.4–1.9 ms) are **two different datasets** (A2) composed as if one system; (iv) the two experiments that could actually kill or save the design — R13 cadence and the replication resource-compile probe (`EXPERIMENT_PLAN §8`; the "one place zero-ingress-cost does not transfer") — are **unrun**, so every co-residency claim is projected/compiler-accepted and explicitly non-monotonic. The permutation-null design is a strength, but its honest consequence is: the current corpus can support a **negative/impossibility** claim (endpoint-stamped + request channels survive) and nothing affirmative.
- **Also:** the "first falsification experiment" (endpoint-stamped header via read-only tshark, §7) is cheap and well-targeted, but the draft **already knows the outcome** (upstream ΔTSval p≈10⁻⁵¹, `ip.id` 82/82, `W(n)=W0−s·n`). A confirmation of a known result is not the decisive test; the decision-critical unknowns are the hardware probes. Do not let the cheap-but-known experiment stand in for the expensive-but-unknown ones in the decision memo.
- **What would answer it:** a data campaign (≥5 independent sessions/device, ≥2 units/model per Open Q7), plus the R13 cadence probe and the resource-compile probe, before any affirmative claim is written. Note this campaign is itself hardware-gated and may not be available.

### O5 — SHOULD-FIX (claim–evidence alignment). The headline overclaims relative to what the proof machinery can certify.

- **Claim attacked:** `README`/`CHARTER` "make the whole observable wire transcript look the same… prove that on real hardware."
- **Why it is wrong:** the proof obligations can, at best, certify invariance over `(K, S_i, d_i, t_i, h_rw)` on the **response direction only, in PATTERN_NORMAL only, with endpoint-stamped fields and the request direction as declared residuals** (`§B.2`, `§0.1`). `O` as defined includes `h_endpoint` and both directions. So the machinery proves a strictly narrower property than the headline. The draft knows this; the abstract/charter must be brought down to it, or the claim is false on arrival at review.

### O6 — SHOULD-FIX (foundation). D4 is solid as a bounded primitive but is documented inconsistently and its live-tested envelope excludes exactly the regimes the new design needs.

- **Is the foundation solid (attack point 6)?** As a bounded timing primitive, yes — the 240/240, 0-bypass result was independently re-derived from raw `blocks.jsonl` (`EVIDENCE_LEDGER` Finding 2; `D4_UPSTREAM_CONTRACT §3`), which is real rigor. But three caveats limit how much load it can bear:
  1. **Documented contradiction (A1).** A same-commit sibling (`research/size_timing_coresidency/CHARTER.md`, dated *newer* than the freeze) and the user auto-memory both still say "defect OPEN, D4 is a mixture, 160/240 held, 80/240 bypass" (pre-fix numbers). The load-bearing foundation is contradicted in-repo; a reviewer or a future self reading the CHARTER would conclude the core does not shape. This must be reconciled before building — inconsistent documentation of the foundation is itself a risk even though the raw evidence sides with the fix.
  2. **Narrow live envelope (Finding 3).** D4 is proven live only for single-segment Case-A READ, one active transaction, **no induced loss/retransmission**, sequential polling. The fixed-transcript design most needs the untested regimes: multi-segment (12,204 B READ), loss/retransmission, concurrency, SELECT/OPERATE — D4's negatives are "offline-validated 58/58, not run live."  Building Class-A correctness on D4's envelope is building past the edge of the live evidence.
  3. **Dataset seam (A2).** Size and timing were measured on different traffic; any co-residency claim inherits the seam.
- **What would answer it:** reconcile the stale CHARTER/memory against the freeze; and treat multi-segment/loss/control as *unproven*, to be re-established live, not inherited.

### O7 — SHOULD-FIX / opportunity. The bounded (Architecture-5) floor is defensible only via the impossibility result or a working N2; as "D4 + header rewrite" it is too incremental for a top venue.

- **Is Architecture 5 a paper on its own (attack point 7)?** Its content is R-time (= D4) + R-header (N1/N2/N3). The genuinely new deltas over frozen D4 are (i) the **TSval/TSecr rewrite (N2)** — never done, and gated on an **unconfirmed** runtime-Δ TCP checksum compile (the draft's own top compile risk, `§7`), and (ii) the **impossibility result**: single-edge plaintext, endpoint-preserving cannot reach a fixed transcript for a self-describing protocol, with the endpoint-stamped and request channels measured as the surviving leaks. The impossibility result grounded in protocol + topology + hardware is the **most novel and defensible thing in the whole draft**. As "D4 + TTL/DF rewrite" alone it is too thin for TDSC/NDSS; as "clean impossibility boundary + the measured achievable floor + a silicon-confirmed N2 timestamp closure" it is a solid mid-tier-to-good paper — but it is a *different paper* from the "provably fixed transcript" the charter headlines.

## Verdicts on the seven attack points

1. **Unavoidability:** **Upheld — no logic gap for the strong property.** There is no single-edge, no-peer design that reaches a fixed transcript; the protocol+topology+hardware chain is sound. The finding is not a hole but a consequence: it collapses the "Tofino defense" into "paired software crypto gateways + a Tofino accessory" (feeds O1). Minor soft spot: the "MACsec is length-preserving, does not help" step is SKU/config-dependent, not absolute.
2. **Cadence (R13):** **Does not force NO_GO by itself, but forces GO_WITH_BOUNDED_CLAIM + a mandatory first silicon probe.** The entire *Tofino-metronome* timing story is unproven (the proven D4 primitive is a different, reactive mechanism). Because a software pacer can substitute, R13 is lethal to the switch-centered framing, not to the defense (feeds O1/O2).
3. **Plaintext-chaff (R14):** **Transport analysis is right; the PRIOR_WORK novelty framing is unsound.** Plaintext CRC-valid chaff is detectable or deletes relay SOE. Salvage is negligible (narrow S6 / decoy-READ, mostly out of scope). MUST strike Z.1/Z.2 (O3).
4. **Novelty:** **Does not clear the bar as framed.** After unavoidability, the design is IP-TFS/Ditto-between-two-gateways applied to DNP3, with the Tofino's only unique addition (line-rate low-jitter cadence) unmeasured. The "novel intersection" is the infeasible cell. Off-the-shelf IP-TFS at a substation crypto gateway plausibly already gives the property, making the contribution marginal unless R13 retires positive and the framing moves to the measured switch-vs-software delta or to the impossibility result.
5. **Proof obligations / evaluation:** **Obligations are correctly specified and rigorous** (equivalence testing, Bayes-optimal chance baseline, PATTERN_NORMAL coverage, mixture-not-median). **But they certify a narrower property than the abstract claims (O5), the first experiment confirms a known result rather than testing the unknown, and the current data (6 flows, p-floor 0.0769, 1 unit/model, two datasets) supports no affirmative invariance claim (O4).** The chance baseline is correct. The evaluation is strong enough to support a *negative* claim now, not a positive one.
6. **D4 dependency:** **Solid as a bounded primitive (re-derived from raw), but documented inconsistently (stale CHARTER/memory) and live-tested only in the regime the new design must leave.** Build on the timing primitive within its envelope; re-establish multi-segment/loss/control live rather than inheriting them (O6).
7. **Bounded claim:** **Defensible only via the impossibility result and/or a silicon-confirmed N2 TSval closure; too incremental as "D4 + header rewrite."** The impossibility framing is the strongest card in the draft.

## Reviewer-stance recommendation

**GO_WITH_BOUNDED_CLAIM.** There is a real, honest, publishable result here — but it is not the one on the cover. The full "provably fixed transcript on hardware" claim is, as framed, either infeasible (single-edge/plaintext, killed by the draft's own R1/R14) or non-novel (paired-gateway IP-TFS/Ditto applied to DNP3) or unproven (R13). The defensible paper is the **impossibility boundary** (single-edge, plaintext, endpoint-preserving cannot reach a fixed transcript for a self-describing, correctness-critical protocol) **plus the measured Architecture-5 floor** (timing + rewritable-header shaping) **plus a silicon-confirmed TSval/TSecr closure (N2)** — with the endpoint-stamped and request-direction channels reported as measured residuals. NO_GO is too harsh (the impossibility result is genuine and grounded); full GO is unjustified (the novel cell is infeasible and the switch's unique value is unmeasured).

**The single condition that would most change this recommendation:** a **positive silicon cadence measurement retiring R13** — showing the Tofino grid delivers a low-jitter, line-rate release that a software IP-TFS pacer demonstrably cannot match *within the DNP3 latency budget*. That single result is the only thing that separates "a novel switch contribution" from "IP-TFS in software with a redundant switch," and it would move the recommendation from GO_WITH_BOUNDED_CLAIM toward GO. Absent it (or if it returns negative/unrunnable), the honest outcome is firmly the bounded impossibility+floor paper.
