## Documented failure mechanism (source-grounded, not narrative):
The non-copyable ICommandTaskResult is marshalled to Python once per SBO; that marshalling
aborts (Py3.12) / hangs (Py3.8). The harness works around it by BLOCKING the single DNP3
thread forever after ONE SBO (block_forever, never set) then os._exit -- inherently one-shot.
