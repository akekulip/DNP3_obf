## 3. Nothing was moved or removed, and why

`CLEANUP_PLAN.md` was written for the 2026-08-24 prune and is labelled historical. This session
proposes **no** moves and **no** removals, so it adds nothing to that plan.

The reason is that the conditions the plan's classifications were for no longer hold. The tree
is already organised as `defense4/timing/` with the exact experiment source immutable, one
canonical evidence tree, and generated outputs rebuilt rather than tracked. The duplicate
`E_FINAL` copies the plan was written to resolve are not in the working tree at all: they were
pruned on 2026-08-24 and exist at the archive tag, where they are proven identical by tree
hash. There is no remaining duplicate, nothing whose only copy would be at risk, and no file
that would be better somewhere else.

New material was placed so that it does not disturb what is already verified: the corrected
harness in its own directory rather than beside the frozen one; the new tools and their outputs
under `audit_current/` rather than in the green reproduction, so `reproduce.sh` and its
publication gate were not perturbed; and the new figures in `figures/model/` rather than in
`paper/rewrite/figures/`, so no manuscript figure changed.
