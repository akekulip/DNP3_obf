### fig_g1_offsets

**Choosing and bounding the two offsets.** (a) fraction of Timing OFF transactions exceeding a given CLRT; (b) transactions not coverable against added latency $D$; (c) sweep of $D_A$ at $D_R=4$ ms; (d) fixed budget $D_A+D_R=24$ ms, split varied.

**Statistics.** Survival and coverage over the 31,680 Timing OFF transactions of 22 sessions; sweep points are medians over 200 to 400 READ transactions each.
