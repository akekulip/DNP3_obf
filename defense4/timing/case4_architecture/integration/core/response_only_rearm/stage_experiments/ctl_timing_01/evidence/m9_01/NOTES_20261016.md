# m9_01: what the N reset-wait correction covers, and what it does not (2026-10-16)

## What m9 is

m9 = m8 + `transform_n_m9`, one N change; E and T are byte-identical to m8.
- **Source:** `6590141f…`.
- **Compile:** N12/E12/T11 on SDK 9.13.1 and 9.13.2, mirror guard PASS.
- **`resource_review.json`:** its `E_byte_identical: false` compares E with candidate61, not with m8. Against m8, E
  is byte-identical (`92b69356…`).

The defect m9 corrects is in `../model_contention_m8_02_02/FAILURE_20261016.md`; the regressions are in
`tests/test_reset_then_syn.py`.
- **Defect:** an event that claims N's work cell between a master RST's stage-0 and stage-1 passes dropped the RST.
  That event was a same-tuple SYN one lap later, reproduced deterministically.
- **Correction:** the RST now waits at stage 1, at most 32 laps, while the cell is busy and the owner and epoch
  are unchanged.

## Covered (source pipeline)

- a SYN one lap after the RST: the RST waits 3 laps, then closes once (the wait path asserted);
- the cell busy for 5, 12 or 31 laps, then free: the RST closes once;
- the cell never freed: the RST aborts after 32 waits, as on m8;
- a duplicate RST during the wait: exactly one reset reaches the outstation;
- no SYN at all: the RST closes once;
- m8 is pinned failing at one lap.

## Open limitations (code review, 2026-10-16)

1. **The wait applies only while the owner is unchanged.** An event whose stage-1 CAS changes the owner still loses
   the RST, as on m8: for example a master READ one lap after its own RST. A compliant master sends nothing after
   its RST. Pinned by `test_m9_open_limitation_an_owner_changing_event_still_loses_the_reset`.
2. **The SYN that met the waiting RST is dropped.** The master reconnects on its SYN retransmission, about 1 s later
   on Linux.
3. **Recirculation cost.** One RST can now take up to 32 stage-0 busy retries plus 32 stage-1 waits, about 65
   passes through N's port 68. A sustained SYN stream that keeps the cell busy for all 32 waits ends in m8's abort.
4. **An unenforced assumption.** Only N's own recirculation writes a non-zero `event.reserved` (`reset_wait`, at most
   32). This holds today because T's `t_N_RETURN` is unused. The generator does not assert it yet.
