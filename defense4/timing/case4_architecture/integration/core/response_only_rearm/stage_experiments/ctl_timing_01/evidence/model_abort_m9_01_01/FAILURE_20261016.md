# model_abort on m9_01, attempt 01: 76 pass, 3 fail. The failures are acceptance errors in the driver, not candidate defects

RESOLVED: the driver's held-branch acceptance was corrected from the m9 T source (model_contention.py, abort
section). The candidate is unchanged. Attempt 02 runs the corrected acceptance on the same source hash.

## What failed and why

1. **`abort_held_READ` and `abort_held_SELECT`.**
   - Everything the case checks on the wire was correct:
     - one RST to the master at s+g, its ACK equal to c, valid IPv4 and TCP checksums;
     - no response to the master;
     - E outcome 16 up by exactly 1.
   - The case also required a T flush counter (HELD_STALE_FLUSH or HELD_OFF_FLUSH). T instead counted the
     discarded held response as **OLD_EPOCH_DROP** (+1).
   - Source (m9 T): N's close runs T's `close_epoch`, so the held response's next pass has `e_bad` set, and
     `held_verdict`'s last row, `(_, …) : drop_old_epoch()` (line 2435), drops it and counts it.
   - The expectation named the wrong counter. The behaviour is the intended discard.
2. **`abort_held_OPERATE`.**
   - The response was already released before the RST (`held_before_rst` False). The RST then landed at s+g+61,
     one response reached the master, and E-16 did not move. That is the released branch, exactly.
   - Source (m9 T): T admits t0 when the OPERATE command arrives (`t0_access`, `(t_T_IN, t_KIND_OPERATE, _, _) :
     admit_t0()`, line 1783) and holds the command for J. J is 200 ms in the model, more than D, which is 1 ms. The
     response is therefore due when it arrives and is never held.
   - The same ordering holds on silicon (J > D), so "OPERATE abort while its response is held" is not a reachable
     state of the supported profile.
   - The corrected case requires that the response was observed released before the RST, and then the
     released-branch outcome.

Every other case passed:
- the released branch for READ, SELECT and OPERATE;
- the negatives: an RST 48 or 50 octets ahead while a response is held does not reset the master, and the held
  response is then released once;
- the closing READ on the same tuple.

The negatives at 49 and 37 with nothing held are recorded in `meta.abort_negative_characterization`. In both, the
RST landed at the frontier after the released response, and E-16 did not move. With nothing held, the frontier is
already the RST's position, so the new rows did not fire.
