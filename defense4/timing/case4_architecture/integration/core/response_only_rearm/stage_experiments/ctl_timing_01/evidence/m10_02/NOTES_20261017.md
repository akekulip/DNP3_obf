## Open items

1. **OPEN (inherited from m9; not introduced by m10).** `test_mutated_retry_form_never_claims[mutation3]` passes on
   m8_02 and fails on m9 and m10. A forged 0x0400 retry frame with `reserved = 1` on RETURN_PORT now reaches the
   master port. The cause is m9's widening of reserved acceptance to 0–63 (parser and later-stage guard). m10
   relies on that widening for its shared lap counter. RETURN_PORT is fed only by the switch's own recirculation,
   so the frame is not reachable from outside; it is still a defect in the retry-form validation, and not fixed here.
2. **Kept limitation.** A master that sends a segment after its own RST: m10 restarts the RST once and its stage-0
   pass refuses it on SEQ (`test_m10_open_limitation_a_master_sending_after_its_own_reset`). A compliant master
   sends nothing after its RST. This is not the response race m10 corrects.
